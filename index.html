Design principles — Chris Domina, July 23, 2026

  • Behavior over intent. Rate what they do (response speed, co-creation, contracting), not what they say.
  • Sponsorship is proximity to real power, not title. "Needs to sell it internally" is a double red flag.
  • Distance from core strategy predicts delivery failure. Senior-leader time is non-negotiable.
  • Relationship pipeline ≫ RFP (IDEO: ~70% vs ~15% win rates). Co-creating the proposal predicts collaboration quality.
  • Don't over-filter at PMF stage. Use the score to prioritize the funnel and target fixes — not to shrink it.
  • Re-score at every touchpoint. The trend of the score is more predictive than any single reading.
